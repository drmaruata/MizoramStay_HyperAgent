# Product Requirements Document (PRD)
## Mizoram Tourism Marketplace & Homestay Platform
Working Product Name: MizoramStay
Product Category: Tourism Marketplace / Accommodation OTA / Homestay Management Platform
Geographic Focus: Mizoram, India
Initial Market: Domestic tourists visiting Mizoram
Secondary Market: Foreign tourists, corporate/institutional travellers, tour operators and travel agencies
Document Version: 1.0
Product Stage: Concept → MVP
Business Model: Marketplace commission + SaaS + tourism services
Primary Platforms: Web + Progressive Web App
Future Platforms: Android / iOS applications

# 1. Executive Summary
Mizoram is developing into a more significant tourism destination within Northeast India, supported by increasing tourist arrivals, destination-development initiatives, improved connectivity, government investment and growing interest in nature, culture, adventure and community-based tourism.
Reported tourist arrivals increased substantially during FY 2024–25, reaching approximately 524,784 visitors, including 518,873 domestic and 5,911 foreign visitors.
The Government of Mizoram is also actively promoting homestays and rural tourism. The state’s homestay framework establishes eligibility, registration, documentation and classification requirements, while recent tourism initiatives include government-supported homestay development and design initiatives.
At the national level, the Ministry of Tourism has also introduced initiatives around rural and tribal homestays under Swadesh Darshan 2.0, indicating policy support for community-based accommodation and destination development.
However, the opportunity should not be approached as simply another accommodation directory.
The proposed product will become a digital tourism marketplace for Mizoram, beginning with verified homestays and subsequently expanding into:
Hotels and guesthouses
Homestays
Village stays
Tour packages
Local experiences
Guides
Taxi and transport
Trekking and adventure activities
Cultural experiences
Restaurants and local food
Events and festivals
Travel planning
Tourism information
AI-powered itinerary generation
The strategic objective is:
Become the digital starting point for travellers planning, booking and experiencing Mizoram.

The platform should also address structural problems around:
Fragmented accommodation information
Lack of standardized property data
Difficulty discovering lesser-known destinations
Limited digital visibility for rural properties
Inconsistent accommodation information
Limited tourism demand analytics
Lack of standardized verification
Limited digital tools for small tourism operators
The platform should complement government tourism infrastructure rather than attempt to replace the government’s regulatory role.
Mizoram already has government tourism booking and digital tourism initiatives, including online tourist-lodge booking and other tourism-related digital services.
Therefore the product’s differentiation should be:
Government = regulation, destination development and official tourism administration.
Platform = discovery, marketplace, booking, payments, experiences, customer acquisition and tourism technology.

# 6. Product Principles
The platform should follow seven principles.
### 6.1 Trust First
Every property should have a clear verification status.
### 6.2 Local First
The platform should prioritize local operators and community participation.
### 6.3 Mobile First
A significant percentage of hosts will primarily use smartphones.
### 6.4 Simple for Hosts
A property owner should be able to register and manage a property without technical knowledge.
### 6.5 Data Driven
The platform should collect structured tourism and accommodation data.
### 6.6 Responsible Tourism
The platform should encourage sustainable, community-based and environmentally responsible tourism.
### 6.7 Scalable Architecture
The system should eventually support other Northeast states without requiring a complete rebuild.

# 45. Illustrative Business Scenario
These are planning assumptions, not forecasts.
Suppose:
500 active properties
Average:
3 rooms/property
Average room rate:
₹1,500
Average occupancy attributable to platform:
20%
Approximate monthly GMV:
500 × 3 × 30 × 20% × ₹1,500
= ₹13.5 crore
At a 10% platform take rate:
= ₹1.35 crore/month
This illustrates why the business should optimize for transactions, not registrations.
A thousand inactive properties are less valuable than 200 properties generating consistent bookings.

# 53. Competitive Strategy
The competitive landscape should be viewed as four groups:
### Global OTAs
Airbnb, Booking.com and similar platforms.
Strength:
Large user bases.
Weakness:
Limited Mizoram-specific specialization.
### Indian OTAs
MakeMyTrip, Goibibo and others.
Strength:
Large domestic customer base.
Weakness:
Mizoram is one destination among thousands.
### Government Tourism Platforms
Strength:
Official information and government legitimacy.
Weakness:
Marketplace and transaction optimization may not be their primary objective.
### Regional Specialists
Regional operators can provide local knowledge and specialized services. Fursat, for example, explicitly targets Northeast India and hill destinations and offers accommodation distribution across multiple channels.
Therefore the platform must compete on:
Local depth + verification + complete journey + community supply.

## Dynamic / Static Site Strategy
The site is a hybrid dynamic web application.
Static/cached public content: destination pages, travel guides, FAQs, policies and editorial content.
Dynamic content: search availability, pricing, booking, payment, user accounts, host dashboards and admin dashboards.
Hybrid property pages: descriptive content and SEO metadata may be statically/cached rendered, while current availability/pricing is fetched dynamically.
Therefore the product is neither a conventional static website nor a purely server-rendered application. It is a Next.js application combining SEO-friendly rendering with dynamic marketplace functionality.
## Updated Technical Acceptance Criteria
- Vercel deploys the Next.js application through development, preview and production environments.
- Supabase Auth supports secure tourist, host and administrator sessions.
- RLS tests demonstrate that tourists and hosts can access only data permitted for their role and ownership scope.
- Sensitive verification documents are stored privately and are never publicly readable.
- Booking inventory changes are transaction-safe and protected against concurrent double booking.
- Payment completion is driven by verified payment-provider webhooks.
- Cancellation/refund workflows are idempotent.
- Important host booking/status changes can be delivered through Realtime or notification workflows.
- Scheduled reminders and asynchronous notification jobs can be executed without adding a standalone job server.
- SEO pages remain crawlable and fast while live booking data stays dynamic.
